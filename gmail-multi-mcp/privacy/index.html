Privacy Policy

gmail-multi-mcp · Effective 27 October 2026

This policy explains what the gmail-multi-mcp application ("the app") does with information, including information it receives from Google APIs. The app is operated by Valantiq Limited ("we"). It is a personal tool that runs on its owner's own computer, and we do not operate any service that receives users' email.

1. Information the app accesses

When its owner signs in with Google and approves access, the app can read, through the Gmail API and the single read-only scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly:

  • email messages and conversations (headers and text, and the names, types and sizes of attachments, never the attachment files);
  • labels and existing drafts;
  • the email address of the signed-in mailbox.

The app cannot send, delete, modify, label or archive anything. It requests no other Google permission.

2. How the information is used

The app reads email only when the owner asks their AI assistant to search or read mail, and uses it only to return the result of that request to the owner. We do not use Google user data for advertising, for profiling, or to build any database, and we do not sell it.

3. Where the information goes

  • No transmission to us. Email content is processed on the owner's computer. We do not run a server that receives it, and the app does not send it to us.
  • The owner's AI assistant. The text the app returns goes to the AI assistant application the owner uses (for example Claude Desktop or Claude Code). That assistant sends the text it is given to its model provider so it can answer, under that provider's own terms and privacy policy and at the owner's direction. We do not control that processing.
  • Google. The app communicates with Google's servers to sign in and to read mail.
  • We do not otherwise share, sell or transfer Google user data to anyone, except where required by law.

4. What is stored, and where

  • Sign-in credential. A refresh token is stored on the owner's computer, in the operating-system keychain where available, otherwise in a file readable only by the owner. Short-lived access tokens are held in memory only.
  • Account list. A local file lists the owner's account nicknames and email addresses. It contains no secrets.
  • Activity log. A local log records which tool was used, when, whether it succeeded, the nickname of the account, the number of results and how long the request took. It never contains the content, subjects, senders or search terms of any email.
  • The app does not keep email content after answering a request.

5. Limited Use

gmail-multi-mcp's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular:

  • we use Google user data only to provide the user-facing read and search features the owner asks for;
  • we do not transfer it to others except as needed to provide those features (as described in section 3), for security or legal reasons, or with the owner's consent;
  • we do not use it for serving advertisements;
  • we do not allow humans to read it, except with the owner's consent, for security purposes, or to comply with law;
  • we do not use it to develop, improve or train generalized AI or machine-learning models.

6. Retention, deletion and revoking access

The app keeps no email content. To remove an account, the owner can run gmail-multi-mcp auth remove <account>, which removes the stored credential and revokes it at Google. Deleting the configuration folder (~/.config/gmail-multi-mcp by default) removes all of the app's local files (the account list and activity log as well) but not a keychain entry. Access can also be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

7. Security

Credentials are stored in the keychain or in owner-only files, the app communicates with Google over encrypted connections, it logs no credentials or email content, and, while running as an assistant tool, it opens no network port. During the one-time sign-in it briefly listens on this computer only (127.0.0.1) and closes after Google redirects back. No system is perfectly secure, and the app's safety also depends on the security of the owner's own computer.

8. Children

The app is not directed to children and is not intended for anyone under 16.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page. Material changes will be made before the app's use of Google data changes.
