From individual AI excellence to an AI-first enterprise

Built to cut cost

I built valantiq.com alone with Claude Code. Every decision sits in a knowledge graph that builds itself, automatically, through Graphify, a Claude Code skill, just by chatting.

I was the one owner of everything that leadership, product, content, design, engineering, data and analytics, and legal and privacy teams would normally own. So there was no conflict. The AI needed up to 99 percent fewer tokens to answer a question or do a task.

That works for one person. It does not work for a team, because nothing in it is owned or governed.
